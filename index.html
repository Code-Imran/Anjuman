Official Message

Welcome to Anjuman-I-Islam Public School & Jr. College, Panchgani. For over a century, our institution has stood as a beacon of quality education, moral uprightness, and holistic development in the serene hills of Maharashtra.

Our mission is to nurture confident, compassionate, and intellectually curious young men and women who are grounded in ethical values while prepared to excel on global platforms.

Through state-of-the-art academic curricula, dedicated faculty mentorship, and an enriching residential campus life, we empower every student to realise their highest human and scholarly potential.

Adv. Shaukat Ali Betgeri

Advocate & Educationist Executive Chairman, Board of Maharashtra Public Schools
Official Message

At Anjuman-I-Islam Panchgani, education extends far beyond textbooks. It is an immersive journey of character building, self-reliance, and physical vitality fostered within an inspiring mountain setting.

We provide rigorous preparation across Maharashtra State Board (SSC and HSC Science/Commerce streams), alongside coaching for NEET, JEE, and competitive national examinations.

Our focus remains steadfast on providing a safe, disciplined, and modern residential atmosphere where students from across India and abroad discover their true talents.
