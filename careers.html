Our Heritage & Environment

A Century of Educational Distinction

Established in 1920, Anjuman-I-Islam Public School & Jr. College carries over a century of educational tradition set against the scenic hills of Panchgani. With our comprehensive academic structure spanning foundational schooling through to our Junior College offering both Science and Commerce streams, we provide an intellectually stimulating environment designed for scholastic rigor and personal growth. Our institution prides itself on cultivating a supportive, inclusive work culture where dedicated educators and professional staff collaborate harmoniously. Members of our team enjoy an enriching community life, opportunities for professional advancement, and the rewarding privilege of mentoring students within a celebrated residential campus.
