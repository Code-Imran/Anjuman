The Boarding Experience

Why Choose Us?

Nestled in a peaceful hill station, our students thrive as part of a close-knit community focused on personalized guidance, academic mastery, and physical vitality.

At Anjuman-I-Islam Public School & Jr. College, Panchgani, academic progress is a core commitment. As the school year unfolds, teachers, advisors, and administrators work collaboratively to ensure every student thrives in their scholastic pursuits.

Outside of the classroom, academic advisors meet regularly with students to set measurable goals, track milestones, and offer one-on-one mentorship. Leadership continuously monitors academic performance, using structured feedback to implement timely, targeted academic interventions.

Our holistic approach combines a caring residential environment with rigorous preparation for academics, competitive entrance examinations, and sports—setting every student on an enduring path to success while keeping personal well-being paramount.

100+ Years Of Heritage

Brief History & Campus Heritage

Anjuman-I-Islam Public School & Jr. College, Panchgani spans 174 acres of verdant mountain terrain, home to diverse regional flora and fauna that beautifies the historic campus. Along with primary and secondary schooling and our Junior College (offering Science and Commerce streams), we provide coaching for NEET, JEE, MH-CET, and NATA. Dedicated academic assistance is thoughtfully extended to students from vernacular medium backgrounds needing structured bridge training.

To keep learning engaging and future-focused, we integrate cutting-edge experiential modules including Robotics, Agriculture, and Basic Aviation courses alongside cherished heritage activities such as equestrian horse riding, pottery, and archery.

With more than a century of providing distinguished education, Anjuman Public School Panchgani continues to foster global citizenship, academic excellence, and integrity in every child.
